# PAPER-10 local release diff summary

The candidate change is limited to the Paper Workspace release surface:

- `backend/paper_processor/client.py`: fixed Edge control-plane validation and
  unique boot/process/nonce instance identity generation.
- `backend/paper_processor/delivery.v1.json`: versioned `v2` zhangbot runtime,
  artifact/hash, environment, secret, singleton, hardening, health, logging,
  and rollback contract.
- `backend/paper_processor/infinity-paper-processor.service`: hardened
  systemd-user unit with no listener and no embedded secret.
- `backend/requirements.paper-processor.zhangbot.txt`: exact dependency pins.
- `cloudflare-worker/wrangler.jsonc`: non-secret
  `PAPER_PROCESSOR_ID=paper-processor-zhangbot-v1` binding.
- `tests/test_paper_processor_runtime.py` and the delivery test: positive and
  negative runtime-contract coverage.
- `docs/CLOUDFLARE_PAPER_WORKSPACE_DESIGN.md`,
  `docs/CLOUDFLARE_PAPER_WORKSPACE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`, and the Processor
  runbook: synchronized zhangbot-only deployment definition and operations.

The existing PubMed PMID abstract-only contract and
`PAPER_APPROVED_URL_DISABLED` entry-point rejection remain unchanged and are
covered by the full Edge suite. C7 Worker v2, D1/R2 schema SQL, Redis, Relay,
Cloudflared, frontend product code, and external systems were not altered.

No `deployment.txt` is present yet: the required read-only Cloudflare and
zhangbot preflight, migrations, secret handoff, service installation, deploy,
and authenticated live acceptance have not started in this local phase.
