## Key Methodological Findings

- Cold plasma treatment increased mean germination from 18% to 60% across 61 wild Brassica rapa populations
- Plasma reduced time to 10% germination (T10) from 61 to 37 hours and mean germination time (MGT) from 66 to 56 hours
- Greenhouse emergence was not significantly improved by plasma (both ~55%)
- Scarification was most effective (79% germination) but plasma showed more uniform results than GA3 (IQR: 26% vs 40%)
- Algerian and Italian populations showed deeper dormancy associated with thicker, more lignified seed coats
- French populations exhibited non-deep dormancy with thinner seed coats and reticulate-foveolate pattern
- Plasma treatment caused minimal morphological changes but likely modified surface chemistry/permeability
- Standard plasma parameters (10 min, 6 kV, 500 Hz) were not optimized and need population-specific refinement
